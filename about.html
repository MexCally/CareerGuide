ABOUT CAREERGUIDE

Helping people explore what comes next.

CareerGuide is a career-exploration project designed to make it easier to learn about different paths, reflect on interests, and find useful next steps.

Explore careers → Talk with the AI guide

A starting point for exploration—not a test that decides anyone’s future.

◎ Explore with curiosity Compare possibilities, ask questions, and make informed decisions.

WHY WE CREATED IT

Career choices can feel overwhelming.

We created CareerGuide as a clear, approachable place to begin exploring. It brings career information, a short reflection activity, and an AI chat guide together so people can discover options and decide what to investigate further.

01

Make exploration easier

Browse career areas and get a simple introduction to roles, study routes, and useful skills.

02

Encourage reflection

Use the five-question activity to identify sectors that may be worth learning more about.

03

Support next steps

Ask questions, compare ideas, and use the suggestions as a starting point for further research.

WHO IT IS FOR

A starting point for anyone exploring work.

CareerGuide is intended for people who want to understand their options. It can be useful to:

STUDENTS

Explore future possibilities

Learn about career areas while considering subjects, study routes, and skills to build.

GRADUATES

Research career directions

Compare roles and identify questions to ask while planning a first career step.

CAREER CHANGERS

Consider new options

Explore different sectors and find areas for further research or discussion with an adviser.
